Leads capture inbound opportunities before they become proposals. Use this flow when you want to track a contact that is not yet a client request or a job post.

## Step 1 — Open the Leads page

In the left-side navigation, go to **Pipeline → Leads**. You will land on the leads list with search and status filters at the top.

## Step 2 — Click «Add lead»

The primary button in the top-right of the list opens the lead form.

## Step 3 — Fill in the required fields

- **Full name** or **company name** — at least one is required.
- **Email** or **phone** — at least one contact point.
- **Source** — where the lead came from (Upwork, LinkedIn, referral, website…).
- **Status** — defaults to «New»; move it to «In conversation» as you reach out.

## Step 4 — Save

The lead appears at the top of the list. You can convert it to a proposal from its preview page once you decide to move forward.
