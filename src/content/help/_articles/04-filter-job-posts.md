The Job posts list is where AI-ranked opportunities land. Triage them daily — do not scroll through every row.

## The Decision tape

Four pills across the top: **All · Approve · Maybe · Decline**. The AI picks one for every post. Flip to «Approve» first, then «Maybe». Decline is for review of what the AI rejected.

## Row 2 filters

- **Priority** — focus on `High` when you only have 10 minutes.
- **Status** — defaults to `Processed`; change to `New` to see posts the AI is still working on.
- **Score min / Score max** — tighten the range (e.g. 70-100) to catch only strong matches.
- **From / To** — date range. Default is all time; usually last 24-48h is enough.
- **Clear** — one-click reset.

## Opening a post

Click the eye icon to open the preview. The row dot on the title column shows posts you have already looked at (per-user, synced across devices).
