Client calls are tracked separately from proposals so you can see every touch-point in one place.

## From a client request

On the request preview page there is a **Book a call** button in the top-right corner. It opens the call form with the client pre-selected.

## From anywhere else

Go to **Pipeline → Client calls → Add call**. Pick:

- **Client type** — lead, client request, or counterparty.
- **Client** — searchable dropdown.
- **Date & time** — pick a slot.
- **Agenda** — one or two sentences on what the call is about.

A reminder is sent before the call. After the call, open its page and fill in **Notes** and **Outcome** so the next person on the account is up to speed.
