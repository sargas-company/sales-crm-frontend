Proposals are the main artefact in the pipeline. Each proposal is tied to a platform, carries connects/boost settings, and can convert a job post or a lead into an active deal.

## From a job post

If the opportunity came from a scanned job post:

1. Open **Pipeline → Job posts**, find the row, click the eye icon.
2. On the job post preview, press **Convert to proposal**.
3. The form opens with the job URL, title and raw text pre-filled.

## From scratch

Go to **Pipeline → Proposals → Add proposal**. Fill in:

- **Title** — short description of the project you are bidding on.
- **Platform** — Upwork, LinkedIn, direct, …
- **Vacancy** — the raw job description.
- **Proposal type** — `Bid` (uses connects) or `Direct offer`.
- **Boosted** + **Connects** — only when proposal type is `Bid`.

## Save and send

Save first. Edit as many drafts as you want. When you are ready, use the Upwork extension to send; the status updates in the CRM when a response comes in.
